Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026.

Bond respects the people who visit its house. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the control you keep. We built the site to know as little about you as possible, because discretion is not a feature of this brand; it is the brand.

1. Who we are

This site is operated by Cedargrowth LLC, a New York State licensed cannabis business (OCM-PROC-25-000329), the company behind Bond ("Bond," "we," "us"). Contact: [PRIVACY EMAIL].

2. Age verification, first and always

New York law requires this site to verify that every visitor is 21 or older before viewing. The 30-day flag is set only when you click Yes, and a yes flag and its timestamp are kept in localStorage on this device for 30 days, under the name bond_age_ok. It isn't a cookie, nothing is sent, and a No stores nothing. We do not store your date of birth.

When you click Yes, the gate also keeps the time of that click in this tab's session storage, and it clears when you close the tab. An old session flag is deleted on load.

3. What we collect

  • Bond Haus: the Home Haus form stores nothing and sends nothing. If you opt in on the Haus door or on your Haus page, Bond keeps your email, the time you opted in, and the source, so Bond can send Bond Haus updates. That opt in is separate from a product request and it starts unticked. Bond accepts that opt in only when this server has your 21 or older attestation for that email.
  • Dispensary Login: dispensary name, address, contact name, phone number, licensed partner email, New York OCM license number, a hashed password, 21+ confirmation, and account status. The 21+ confirmation is a self-attestation. Bond stores those fields in Supabase, in dispensary_accounts. The password hash is a salted scrypt hash. An owner with an active row in the admins table can read it. A visitor cannot. Passwords are stored hashed only. They are never logged in plaintext and are never sent to Metrc. Creating access does not grant request rights until Bond operations elevates the account. Account status stays on the server. The browser cookie bond_order_partner is only a signed session id, kept for one day. It does not store the password, the status, or the approval. Product requests are stored by Bond in Supabase, linked to that dispensary account. A browser copy of a filed draft may also sit in the cookie and localStorage key bond_order_drafts. That copy is not the approval.
  • Dispensary finder: a location you choose to share is used for the lookup and is not stored.

Haus member records

Bond does not sell member data.

These are the records a Haus member can leave with Bond, what each one is for, how long it is kept, and how you take it back.

  • Email. Purpose: to open the Haus door and to recognize your session. On the Next door the session cookie is a signed id kept for one day, or until you sign out. The email itself is stored by Bond in Supabase, with the dispensary you request, when you confirm you are 21 or older. That confirmation is a self-attestation. On the Haus floor the email is kept in the page until you refresh or erase the whole account.
  • Password. Purpose: to open the Haus door. The Haus floor does not keep the password after you submit the form. Your Haus password is kept by the account service. This policy does not set a deletion period for that password.
  • Authenticator code. Haus members are not asked for an authenticator code. Bond staff who manage the Haus can be asked for a six digit code from an authenticator app when they sign in. That step is switched off for now, so no code is asked for or stored. Each staff sign-in, and each staff page a staff member opens, creates a record of the staff member's account id and role, a keyed hash of the IP address, the browser and page used, and the time. Staff records made before this change keep the IP address until they are deleted at 24 months. Sign-in records also note that the code step was skipped. The staff audit log is deleted after 24 months. Those records are the staff audit log in Supabase. Staff and admin sign-in accounts live in Supabase (auth.users). The sign-in cookies use the sb- prefix. When a staff member sets up an authenticator, the setup image is shown to the staff member's browser and is not kept by Bond. An authenticator that has been set up but not yet confirmed stays with the account service and is reused at the next setup. Bond sets its confirmed flag once, at setup. After it is confirmed, the authenticator stays with the account service with no time limit.
  • First name. Purpose: to greet you on the Haus floor. Kept in the page until you refresh or erase the whole account. The Next door does not ask for a first name.
  • Age 21 confirmation. Purpose: to confirm the Haus is for adults 21 and over. The words on the door are: I confirm I am 21 or older. This is a self-attestation. On the Next door the confirmation, with a note that you have seen the welcome, is stored by Bond in Supabase. Signing out does not delete that row. The Haus request row, including that 21+ self-attestation, is deleted after 24 months. On the Haus floor the confirmation is kept in the page until you refresh or erase the whole account.
  • City. Purpose: to look up a New York city when you ask which licensed dispensaries are near you. Kept in the page until you refresh or erase the whole account.
  • Notes. Purpose: optional notes on a batch you shelve. Kept in the page until you refresh, erase notes, or erase the whole account.
  • Ritual reflections and sessions. Purpose: a date, an intention, the minutes you set, and an optional reflection after a quiet timer. No product is stored with the session. Kept in the page until you refresh, erase reflections, erase ritual history, or erase the whole account. Older sessions that named a product are ignored.
  • Request draft. Purpose: a draft request with your email, the run, the dispensary, the city, and the date. Today this request is not sent to any dispensary or to anyone else. One copy is stored in your browser until you clear site data for this site. A second copy is kept in the page until you refresh, close it, or erase the whole account. Erasing the account does not clear the browser copy.
  • Shelf. Purpose: the batch records you register, aside from the notes above. Kept in the page until you refresh or erase the whole account.
  • Run numbers on your record. Purpose: the run numbers you add from a shelved batch. Kept in the page until you refresh or erase the whole account.
  • Evenings. Purpose: a request or confirmation for a house evening. Kept in the page until you refresh or erase the whole account.
  • Guide place. Purpose: which chapter of The Number Guide you last opened. Kept in the page until you refresh or erase the whole account.
  • Door attempts. Each attempt at the Haus door records a keyed hash of the email entered, a keyed hash of the network address, and whether the attempt succeeded, so repeated attempts can be limited. These rows are deleted after 90 days. The email is stored only as a keyed SHA256 hash (HMAC), together with a keyed SHA256 hash (HMAC) of the IP address, in Supabase sign-in attempts, so the door can lock after repeated failures. The email itself is not stored in that row. The IP address itself is not stored in that row.

From Account on the Haus floor you can download the page record, erase notes, erase ritual reflections, erase ritual history, or erase the whole account. Erasing the account does not remove your password from the account service or the records of door attempts. Signing out clears the one day session cookie. Signing out does not delete the Haus request row. That row, including the 21+ self-attestation, is deleted after 24 months. To remove the browser request copy, clear site data for this site. 4. What we do not collect

We sell nothing on this site, so we keep no purchase records, no payment details, and no government identification. We collect no health information of any kind. We do not buy data about you from anyone. Dispensary Login credentials are stored by Bond in Supabase and are not sent to Metrc. Visitor and partner information from this site is not written into Metrc. Metrc is used for wholesale only.

5. How we use information

To operate the site, to operate Dispensary Login for licensed partners, and to comply with New York cannabis law. The Home Haus form stores nothing and sends nothing. Nothing else. If you opt in on the Haus door or on your Haus page, Bond uses your email for Bond Haus updates. Bond sends no such emails yet. Bond accepts that opt in only when this server has your 21 or older attestation for that email.

6. Sharing

Only with service providers who help us run the site, each bound to our instructions; with the Office of Cannabis Management or other authorities where the law requires; and never sold, and never shared for cross-context behavioral advertising. This site does not use analytics.

Vercel hosts the site. When your browser loads a page, Vercel receives your IP address and browser information and keeps its own request logs. This policy does not set how long Vercel keeps those logs. Home, the product pages, and the age gate page load React and ReactDOM from unpkg.com. Your browser sends your IP address and browser information to unpkg.com when those scripts load. Fonts and video are files we host ourselves.

7. Cookies

The age check is a yes flag and its timestamp, kept in localStorage on this device for 30 days, under the name bond_age_ok. The 30-day flag is set only when you click Yes. It isn't a cookie, nothing is sent, and a No stores nothing. When you click Yes, the gate also keeps the time of that click in this tab's session storage, and it clears when you close the tab. An old session flag is deleted on load. There are no analytics cookies.

These localStorage keys stay in this browser:

  • bondDispensaryDir: the Finder dispensary list.
  • bondNyCities: the Finder city list.
  • bondFindResult: the Home quiz result.
  • bond_order_drafts: a browser copy of a filed request. It is not the dispensary account and it is not the approval. The Finder and quiz keys are not sent to Bond.

Cookies this site sets:

  • bond_order_partner: the Dispensary Login session. It is a signed session id only. The browser keeps it for one day. It does not store the password, the account status, or the approval. Signing out clears it. An older bond_order_accounts cookie is cleared and is not read.
  • bond_order_drafts: a browser copy of a filed request, kept for 30 days. localStorage uses the same name. It is not the approval. The request itself is stored by Bond in Supabase.
  • bond_haus_member: the Haus session. It is a signed session id only. The browser keeps it for one day. Signing out clears it. It does not store Haus membership. Membership is the Supabase request row. An older bond_haus_ack cookie is cleared and is not read.
  • bond_haus_ledger: the Haus page record kept for this browser. The browser keeps it for 30 days.
  • bond_idle_at: the time of the last staff request, used to end a staff session after a day of no use. The browser keeps it for one day. It is set only while a staff member is signed in.
  • sb- cookies: the staff and admin sign-in session. The name starts with sb-, then the Supabase project reference, then -auth-token. A long value may be split into numbered pieces of that name. These cookies are the Supabase sign-in session. They are not a visitor profile.

The Home Haus email box on the home page stores nothing and sends nothing. Your browser controls let you clear site data.

The dispensary name and the New York OCM license number stay as the business record. Product requests and Haus requests, including the 21+ self-attestation, are deleted after 24 months. The bond_order_partner and bond_haus_member cookies are signed session ids and clear when you sign out or when the day ends. They are not the approval and they are not membership.
