Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026.

Bond respects the people who visit its house. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the control you keep. We built the site to know as little about you as possible, because discretion is not a feature of this brand; it is the brand.

1. Who we are

This site is operated by Cedargrowth Organics LLC, a New York State licensed cannabis business (OCM-Proc-25-000329), the company behind Bond ("Bond," "we," "us"). Contact: [PRIVACY EMAIL].

2. Age verification, first and always

New York law requires this site to verify that every visitor is 21 or older before viewing. When you verify, we store a verification flag in a cookie on your device for 30 days, together with your consent to view cannabis-related material. We do not store your date of birth.

3. What we collect

  • Bond Haus: your email address, your confirmation that you are 21 or older, the page you joined from, and the time you joined. New York requires the age confirmation for cannabis email marketing; we keep the record of it and nothing more.
  • Dispensary Login: dispensary name, address, contact name, phone number, licensed partner email, New York OCM license number, a hashed password, 21+ confirmation, and elevation state. Passwords are stored hashed only. They are never logged in plaintext and are never sent to Metrc. Creating access does not grant order rights until Bond operations elevates the account.
  • Site analytics: aggregated usage data (pages viewed, approximate region, device type), configured to avoid building individual profiles.
  • Dispensary finder: a location you choose to share is used for the lookup and is not stored.
  • Bond Haus accounts: if member accounts launch, we will collect what is needed to operate them, and this policy will be updated before that day.

Haus member records

Bond does not sell member data.

These are the records a Haus member can leave with Bond, what each one is for, how long it is kept, and how you take it back.

  • Email. Purpose: to open the Haus door and to recognize your session. On the Next door it is kept in a cookie for one day, or until you sign out. On the Haus floor it is kept in the page until you refresh or erase the whole account.
  • Password. Purpose: to open the Haus door. The Haus floor does not keep the password after you submit the form. Your Haus password is held by our account service. Bond has no deletion process for it yet, so it is kept until one is in place.
  • First name. Purpose: to greet you on the Haus floor. Kept in the page until you refresh or erase the whole account. The Next door does not ask for a first name.
  • Age 21 confirmation. Purpose: to confirm the Haus is for adults 21 and over. On the Next door the confirmation, with a note that you have seen the welcome, is kept in a cookie for one year. Signing out does not clear that cookie. On the Haus floor the confirmation is kept in the page until you refresh or erase the whole account.
  • City. Purpose: to look up a New York city when you ask which licensed dispensaries are near you. Kept in the page until you refresh or erase the whole account.
  • Notes. Purpose: optional tasting notes on a batch you shelve. Kept in the page until you refresh, erase notes, or erase the whole account.
  • Ritual reflections and sessions. Purpose: a date, an intention, the minutes you set, and an optional reflection after a quiet timer. No product is stored with the session. Kept in the page until you refresh, erase reflections, erase ritual history, or erase the whole account. Older sessions that named a product are ignored.
  • Reserve queue. Purpose: a draft request with your email, the run, the dispensary, the city, and the date. Today this request is not sent to any dispensary or to anyone else. One copy is stored in your browser until you clear site data for this site. A second copy is kept in the page until you refresh, close it, or erase the whole account. Erasing the account does not clear the browser copy.
  • Correspondence preference. Purpose: whether you want Haus correspondence. Kept in the page until you refresh or erase the whole account.
  • Shelf. Purpose: the batch records you register, aside from the notes above. Kept in the page until you refresh or erase the whole account.
  • Run numbers on your record. Purpose: the Reserve run numbers you add from a shelved batch. Kept in the page until you refresh or erase the whole account.
  • Evenings. Purpose: a request or confirmation for a house evening. Kept in the page until you refresh or erase the whole account.
  • Guide place. Purpose: which chapter of the Experience Guide you last opened. Kept in the page until you refresh or erase the whole account.
  • Door attempts. Each attempt at the Haus door records a hashed form of the email entered, the network address, and whether the attempt succeeded, so repeated attempts can be limited. These records are kept for members and nonmembers alike until a deletion process is in place.

From Account on the Haus floor you can download the page record, erase tasting notes, erase ritual reflections, erase ritual history, or erase the whole account. Erasing the account does not remove your password from the account service or the records of door attempts. Signing out clears the one day session cookie. To remove the browser reserve queue, clear site data for this site. To remove the one year age confirmation cookie, clear cookies for this site or wait for it to expire.

4. What we do not collect

We sell nothing on this site, so we hold no purchase records, no payment details, and no government identification. We collect no health information of any kind. We do not buy data about you from anyone. Dispensary Login credentials stay on Bond systems and are not sent to Metrc.

5. How we use information

To operate the site, to operate Dispensary Login for licensed partners, to comply with New York cannabis law, to send Bond Haus correspondence you asked for, and to measure what is working. Nothing else.

6. Sharing

Only with service providers who help us run the site (hosting, email delivery, analytics), each bound to our instructions; with the Office of Cannabis Management or other authorities where the law requires; and never sold, and never shared for cross-context behavioral advertising.

7. Cookies

The age and consent verification flag, session cookies necessary for the site to function, and analytics cookies. Your browser controls let you clear or block cookies; blocking the verification cookie means you will verify on each visit, because the law requires the gate, not the cookie.

8. Your choices and rights

Every Bond Haus email includes an unsubscribe link that works the first time. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information; send requests to [PRIVACY EMAIL] and we will verify and respond within the time your state's law requires, and we honor reasonable requests regardless of state.

9. Retention

Bond Haus records, including the required age confirmation, are kept while you are subscribed and as long as New York law requires us to retain marketing compliance records, then deleted. Dispensary Login records are kept while the partner account is active and as long as New York business and cannabis recordkeeping requires, then deleted or disabled when operations revokes the account. Analytics persist in aggregate. The verification cookie expires on its own.

10. Security

Access controls, encryption in transit, and a least-access, audited internal environment, consistent with the New York SHIELD Act's reasonable safeguards requirement. We design so a breach would find very little to take.

11. Under 21

This site is for adults 21 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21, and we delete any such record on discovery.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date changes with it, and material changes will be noted plainly on this page.
