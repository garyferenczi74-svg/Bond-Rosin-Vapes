## Typography
- Headers: GFS Didot, Regular 400 only. Never synthetically bolded.
- Supporting text: Aptos

## Usage rules
- Ink variants on light surfaces for accent text at or under 18 px
- Accents as hairlines, never fills
- One SKU accent per surface
- Radius 0 to 4 px
- Wordmark tracking 0.35em minimum

## Motion
- Signature Moments only (Arrival, Press, Three Worlds) as loud gestures
- Everything else: opacity plus 12 px rise, 400 to 600 ms, cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)
- Reduced motion: opacity only
