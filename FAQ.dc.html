Questions, answered quietly.

What is Bond?

A solventless live rosin house, licensed in New York State. Three numbers: daytime, evening, and the Reserve Edition. Made for adults who notice the difference between made well and made fast.

What is solventless live rosin?

Cannabis, fresh frozen at harvest, washed in ice water, and pressed with heat and patience. No chemical solvents at any step. What the plant made, exactly as it made it.

What makes Bond different?

Five zeros. No chemical solvents. Nothing else added. No distillate. No fillers. No additives.

What do the numbers mean?

No. 1 DIALED is the daytime number. No. 2 UNWIND is the evening number. No. 3 PEAK is the Reserve Edition. Each number is solventless live rosin.

What is a Reserve Edition?

A limited pressing of a single small-batch cultivar, numbered by run. When a run ends, it ends.

Where can I buy Bond?

Only at licensed New York dispensaries. The Find Your Bond page shows the nearest one, and you can confirm any shop's license with the OCM Dispensary Verification Tool. We do not sell through this site, and no one can lawfully ship our products to you from a website.

Is Bond tested?

Every batch is tested by a licensed independent laboratory, and every unit carries its batch number with a certificate of analysis available through the batch lookup. Every Bond is accountable.

Is cannabis risk-free?

No, and we will not pretend otherwise. Cannabis can be addictive. Cannabis can impair concentration and coordination. Do not operate a vehicle or machinery under the influence of cannabis. There may be health risks associated with consumption of this product. Cannabis is not recommended for use by persons who are pregnant or nursing. If someone accidentally consumes cannabis, contact the Poison Center at 1-800-222-1222 or call 9-1-1.

Do I need to be 21?

Yes. This site, and everything Bond makes, is for adults 21 and over, full stop. Keep every cannabis product out of reach of children and pets.

How should I store it?

In its original packaging, upright, cool, and dark, locked away from children and pets. Treat it like anything else you value, and then a little more carefully than that.
